ABOUT

I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Northwestern University. I did my undergraduate studies at Boston University, majoring in Physics and minoring in Computer Science and Philosophy.

I am interested in the intersection of art, science, and technology. While I currently simulate materials at the atomistic level, I’m also drawn to explore materials in tactile ways; currently fixated on learning wheelthrowing with clay and experimenting with glazes on fired ceramics.

In my free time I like to hike, read, or act in student films on campus. Pictured: on the Salkantey trek to Machu Picchu.

RESEARCH

Biophysics — Gold Nanorods & Reactive Oxygen Species

I have been involved with biophysics research throughout undergrad under Professor Erramilli at Boston University. I received funding from UROP to conduct a project and present at a symposium in October 2022. I was involved in implementation and evaluation of the experiment, collaborating with graduate student Ramprasath Rajagopal. Our experiment was a pivot from using a fluorescence marker to studying the degradation of the fluorescence by reactive oxygen species generated by irradiated gold nanorods.

Chaotic Periods of Water Droplets

As an intern in Advanced Laboratory (PY581), I conducted an experiment with my collaborator Zachary Klappenbach studying chaotic periods of water droplets. We used an oscilloscope and photodiodes coordinated by LABVIEW in our setup.

PROJECTS

Predictive AI — White Nose Syndrome in Bat Species

As part of Break Through Tech AI, I worked with 3 undergraduate students to collect climate and bat species data and design a predictive AI model characterizing the spread of White Nose Syndrome in bat species across North America for BioInterphase’s engineering initiative. We presented to stakeholders and challenge advisor Noah Snyder, CEO of BioInterphase, in Dec 2023.

View on GitHub →

MIT Policy Hackathon — Housing Sustainability

In MIT’s Policy Hackathon (2023), my team of 4 undergraduates researched and wrote a policy memo for the housing sustainability challenge put forth by Princeton’s Eviction Lab.
